# Godsim: tribal god-sim sandbox (founders, food, water, sleep, aging and death, animals, things, physics, cold, fire, learning, seasons)

Plain HTML5 canvas and JS. There is no engine, no build step and no modules. Every script is a plain `<script>` tag that hangs off one global, `GS`.

## Run
- **Offline:** double-click `index.html`. It works over `file://`.
- **Local server:** `cd godsim && python3 -m http.server 8000`, then open http://localhost:8000/
- `?seed=1234` in the URL starts that world and ignores the save.

## Controls
- **Pause / 1x / 10x / 60x** buttons (kit icons pause / play / fast / superfast with the label; aria-label and tooltip name the speed and key). Keys: Space = pause/resume, 0 = pause, 1 = 1x, 2 = 10x, 3 = 60x (button tooltips show the key).
- **Click or tap a person** to open the info panel: name, age, activity (or "asleep since HH:MM"), the **Fullness / Water / Energy / Warmth** bars (all read the same way: full = good, the bar fills left to right, with a state word inside: starving/hungry/fed/full/overfull, parched/thirsty/watered, exhausted/tired/fine/rested, freezing/cold/chilly/warm; the Warmth bar's tooltip shows the felt temperature; energy reads "rested" only at 80+), a red "no water / no food / freezing for X h" line, what she is carrying, while a need sits at 0, **Experience** (Fire / Huddling / Shelter scores 0-100, 50 = untried, with how many good times, burns and who she copied from), the traits as integers out of 100 (Speed, Appetite, Curiosity, Cold fear, Fire fear), "Knows" chips (bushes, water, carrying, her own discoveries), and **her own log** at the bottom of the card: her last 200 events, newest first, scrolling at max 40vh (deaths red, births green, discoveries wheat), saved with the world. The selected person shows a sight circle and their path. Use × to close.
- **Click or tap an animal** (grazer or scavenger): species, diet, what it is doing, its Fullness bar, behaviour (flees people within 4 tiles), the nearest person, flights and meals. **Click or tap a body**: whose body, stage (fresh / rotting / bones), cause of death, age at death, time since death, a Flesh bar (fresh/rotting) or the material ("bone") and a Condition bar (bones). Selecting a person who then dies follows on to her body. **Click or tap a thing on the ground** (rock, stick, flint, bone, shard): material, whole or broken piece, on the ground / carried by, whether it burns, and hardness / sharpness / dryness / durability bars. A thing in someone's hand is drawn small over the hand.
- **Clock** also shows the air temperature (°C). People shiver (1 px jitter) when warmth is under 30.
- **Zoom:** + / − buttons (bottom right of the map), mouse wheel (around the cursor) or pinch, in whole steps 1x (whole map) to 4x. Drag to pan; the view is clamped to the map edges. A press that moves more than 8 px is a pan, not a tap.
- **Tab bar** (full-width bar along the bottom of the screen, every size; panels open above it, the map takes the space when none is open): **Log** and **Discoveries**; pressing one opens it (pressing the open one closes it). **Discoveries** lists every first combination of action + materials + result (e.g. "strike flint on rock → spark"), newest first, with #, game date/time, who, and a blank Group column (groups come at step 18). Only firsts are listed. Whether it is open is remembered across reloads in localStorage `godsim.ui` (a UI pref, separate from the save). **Adding a tab is one data entry** in `data/config.js` → `ui.panels`, e.g. `{ key: "discoveries", label: "Discoveries" }`: the button and an empty `<div id="panel-discoveries" class="panel">` are created automatically; to fill it, set `GS.UI.panelRender.discoveries = (app, el) => { el.innerHTML = ... }` (called on each UI update while that panel is open). `el: "<id>"` reuses an existing element instead (Log uses `el: "logwrap"`), `open: true` makes it the open panel on a first visit. One panel is open at a time. **Closing the Log gives the map the freed space** on all sizes (desktop: the card and log float over a full-height map, so closing uncovers it; phone portrait: the map fills the screen above the tab bar; phone landscape: the sheet folds to its handle + tab bar), keeping zoom and a clamped pan. Phone tabs and the sheet handle are ≥ 44 px tap targets; on phone landscape the open sheet state is remembered too (`godsim.ui` `sheet`).
- **Log filters** (pills inside the log panel): All / Food / Water / Sleep / Events / Death (Death = deaths, bodies, bones; Events = everything the other filters don't match). Warnings (hungry, thirsty, starving, parched, exhausted, has died, woke early, bush bare) are marked with a coloured left bar.
- **Save / Load** write and read localStorage key `godsim.save` (versioned JSON, v11, about 45 KB plus up to 200 lines per person's log with 8 founders, 19 animals and their bodies). The game autosaves every 60 real seconds, and also on tab hide and page close. A save from an older version is rejected and a fresh world starts.
- **New world:** type a seed, or leave it blank for a random one, then press New world (or Enter). The current seed is shown in the top bar.
- The world is **paused while closed**. There is no catch-up on return.

## Learning and seasons (steps 13-15)
- **Experience, not scripts:** each person has 0-100 scores per action (`fire`, `huddle`, `shelter`; 50 = untried). Only what she felt moves them: warmth gained near an ember/fire (a cold person within 2 tiles of an ember or 4 of a fire), from huddling or from the trees raises that score (`learn.*.warmGain` per warmth point); stepping onto a burning or smouldering tile is a **burn**: score −35, she drops what she carries and moves away, one log line ("Aru steps into the flames and jumps back, burned"), no damage. Scores scale how strongly each option pulls her when she seeks warmth; the fire one also sets the odds she walks up to an ember (× (score/50)² × (1.5 − fireFear/100)) and whether she stays (score ≥ 30). New trait **fireFear**.
- **Copying:** every 3 warmth points gained is a visible success. An awake person within 5 tiles whose own score is at least 10 lower may copy it: p = 0.35 × curiosity/50 × (1.5 − fear/100 for fire). Her score moves halfway to theirs, tagged with the source; log "Bo watched Aru and copied: huddling up". No language, no tribe knowledge.
- **Seasons:** meal size × 1 / 1.25 / 1.25 / 0.8 and bush regrowth 24 / 18 / 18 / 36 h (Greening / High Sun / Leaffall / Deep Cold); people still expect a bush back after 24 h, so in winter they find more bare. Grazers graze the greenest spot nearby and take twice as long in Deep Cold.

## Map variety (step 15b)
Each seed builds its own map (`config.map`): 3-octave height and moisture noise, a per-seed moisture bias (-0.3 dry .. +0.3 lush), forest density 0.1-0.6 (follows the bias), 0-4 lakes filling low basins, 0-2 rivers running downhill from high ground to a lake or the edge, hills (top 10-15% of height) shown as clusters of the rock thing, very dry open ground as sand. Water is 3-12% of tiles; founders start with water within 12 tiles; at least 15 bushes (dry seeds 30-50% fewer); every walkable tile is reachable. Same tiles and art as before. `GS.debug.seedInfo()` in the console shows lakes, rivers, water %, forest density and moisture bias. Try `?seed=11` (dry), `108` (lush), `235` (rivers), `285` (lakes); `tests/screenshots/15b_seeds.png`.

## Meat and natural fire (step 16)
Hunting is not scripted. A person who is low on fullness and holding something hard (`config.hunt.minHardness` 20: rock, stick, flint, bone) may go after a grazer she sees within 12 tiles. The chance follows her hunt score (0-100, 50 average). She strikes from 4 tiles or less: kill odds are 10%, +30% with a sharp thing (sharpness >= `config.physics.sharpAt` 45, the one threshold also used for "sharp shard": flint or a bone shard), +20% once her hunt score is 60 or more. A kill gives +20 score, a miss -2. Others within 5 tiles can copy it. A kill leaves a grazer carcass that goes fresh -> rotting -> bones like a body (`data/art/grazer_carcass_16.png`: fresh / picked / bones). It holds 4 pieces of meat that can be taken only while it is fresh; the hungry also scavenge fresh carcasses. Raw meat gives +60 fullness over 15 minutes and is edible until the carcass would have rotted, then rots away (`data/art/food_16.png`). One meal in 4 makes her sick (energy -20, hunt score -5). Lightning (`config.fire.lightning`, about 56 strikes per year, most in Leaffall) starts fires by the same spark rule. A struck tree in the woods can smoulder for 2-21 days and rarely flares; its smoke can be seen from 40 tiles.

## Cooking and grazer births (step 17)
Nothing is scripted. Meat heats while it is close to heat (`config.cook`): within 1.5 tiles of an ember or 3 tiles of a fire (people keep 2.5 tiles from flames), held, lying on the ground or still on a fresh carcass. After 30 minutes of heat it is cooked; past 120 it is charred (the raw / cooked / burnt frames of `food_16.png`). Cooked meat gives +70 fullness and never makes anyone sick; charred gives +20; raw stays +60 with a 1-in-4 sickness. A hungry person with raw meat in hand and an ember or fire within 10 tiles may hold it there first (p = 0.15 x (cook score / 50)^2 x (1.5 - fire fear / 100)); she walks next to it, waits until it is cooked (at most 60 minutes, or 10 minutes without heat), then eats it. The first cooked meal is noted in Discoveries. A cooked meal raises her cook score by 20 (others within 5 tiles can copy it); a charred one lowers it by 10. Grazers give birth in Greening (`config.animals.grazer.births`: about one calf per grazer with another within 6 tiles each spring, own random stream), up to 40 grazers and `pop.maxAnimals` 40, so hunting cannot wipe out a herd for good.

## Relations and groups (step 18)
Nothing is scripted (`config.social`, `src/social.js`). Each person keeps a familiarity (0-100) and a liking (0-100, starting at 50) for everyone she has seen. Familiarity rises by 1 per game hour spent within 4 tiles and falls by 1 per game day apart. Liking rises by 2 (once per stretch) when someone is nearby while her fullness, water or warmth is rising (eating, drinking, warming). It falls by 3 when someone takes the bush, meat or fireside spot she was heading for ("X finds Y took the ..."), and it drifts back toward 50 by 1 point every 3 days. When idle, she follows the most-liked person within 15 tiles if that liking is 60+, staying 1-3 tiles off. She keeps 3+ tiles from anyone she likes less than 25. There is no fighting. Once a day, groups are recomputed as connected sets of 2+ people whose mutual familiarity and liking are both 60+. A group keeps its id and seeded colour while more than half of its old members stay together. The log shows "X and Y now keep together", "Z now keeps with ...", "Z no longer keeps with ..." and "... no longer keep together". Copy odds are x1.25 from a group member and x0.85 from anyone else. A group knows every skill a member has scored 40+ in. On screen, each person in a group has a ring in the group colour. The card shows a group row (colour, size, members, what the group knows) and a Relations list: the top 5 by familiarity, labelled warm 70+, neutral 40-69 or wary under 40, with both numbers. The Discoveries Group column now shows the finder's group. Director calls on 17: a wildfire that sweeps a fresh carcass roasts it instead of burning it to bones; a hungry person carrying meat looks up to 15 tiles for a fire; a cold person goes for warmth before she sets off early for water, a far bush or a wander; grazer births are 0.35 per grazer per spring. Smoke art (`smoke_16.png`: wisp and smouldering frames) is drawn over smouldering and burning trees; the base tiles are unchanged.

Display: the sim works in Celsius and minutes; the clock shows a 12-hour time with AM/PM and every air or ground temperature reading is shown in whole °F, all through `G.Time.hhmm` / `G.Time.tempF` (the Warmth meter stays 0-100). Cache-bust: every script/CSS link in `index.html` carries `?v=` equal to `config.saveVersion` (now 14); bump both together (a headless test checks it).

## Burning sticks and families (step 19)
- **Burning sticks** (`config.fire.brand`, `src/fire.js`): a stick in a fire is not consumed; it becomes a burning stick, lit for the burn time + 120 min. It is not fuel and not a plain pickable. An idle person in sight of one away from the flames may take it up with p = 0.5 × (fire score/50)² × (1.5 − fireFear/100) (learning stream), carrying it until it burns out ("X's burning stick burns out"). A lying one can set dry ground or fuel smouldering through the usual ember odds ("The burning stick sets the dry grass smouldering", credited to the last carrier, noted in Discoveries). It gives ember-level heat (1/3).
- **Director calls on 18:** only Warmth under 30 stops a hunt (`hunt.coldBelow`). Following is off in Deep Cold or when any need is under 50 (`social.follow.offSeason/offBelow`). Group rings are a dark 3 px ring under a 2 px colour ring (radii 7/3, 8.5/3.4 asleep), plus a 3×3 dot above the head at zoom 1, in magenta / orange / cyan / yellow / red / white.
- **Families** (`config.family`, `src/family.js`, family stream `frng`):
  - Once a day (6:00 AM, with the groups), adults 16-45 with mutual liking ≥ 75 and familiarity ≥ 70 pair, one partner each, logged once as "A and B stay close by night now". A pair breaks when either liking falls under 40, or on a death.
  - At bedtime a partner walks to a free tile beside the other. At 2:00 AM, if a female and male pair are asleep within 1 tile, both have fullness and water ≥ 35 and warmth ≥ 30, and the population (people + babies + pregnancies) is under 60, she conceives with p = 0.15 (Director, call 1 on 19) ("X is with child"). Same-sex pairs bond but do not conceive.
  - Pregnancy lasts 7 days with fullness drain ×1.25 and ends if she dies. The birth is logged as "X gives birth; a baby lies beside her".
  - The baby's traits and lifespan are the parents' average ±5%, with a 2% chance per trait of a further ±20% mutation. Learned scores are not inherited. It gets a seeded name, the mother's group and a 50/50 sex.
  - The baby is carried on the carrier's tile and its needs follow hers. If the carrier dies, an adult of the group (or a parent) within 2 tiles picks it up; otherwise it dies after a day ("X lies still. X has died of being left alone."). A year is 28 days. At 3 the baby walks (step 20, below).
- **Display:** the baby sprite (`data/art/baby_16.png`) is drawn on the carrier's tile. The card has a Family section (sex, due date, Partner, Parents, Children as 44 px links). A baby's card shows ▲/▼ per trait against the parents' average. Births are in the Events log filter.
- **Save v13**, `?v=13`.
- **Director calls on 19:** conception 15% a night with gates fullness/water ≥ 35, warmth ≥ 30 (above). Every seed starts with ≥ 2 founders of each sex (`family.minFounders`). When the 70-name list runs out a repeat gets " II", " III", ... (`sim.nameCount`). Each warming at a fire adds +10 fire score on top of its heat gain (`learn.fireSuccessGain`). Burning sticks: once a game hour, an awake idle adult by an ember/fire with a burning stick within 3 tiles (away from the flames) takes it with p = 0.05 + 0.1 × (fire score/50)² × (1.5 − fireFear/100). Cooking is left to emerge.

## Childhood (step 20)
- **Stages** (`config.child`, `src/family.js`): baby 0-3 (carried), child 3-12, youth 12-16, adult 16+ (a year = 28 days). Logged as actions: "X walks on her own now, close to Y", "X roams on her own now", "X is grown". The card shows the stage next to the age.
- **Child:** walks alone and keeps within 4 tiles of her guardian (a parent, or an adopted adult) while awake; at bedtime she stays close and lies down beside the guardian (a guardian whose child already fell asleep elsewhere lies down beside her). Fullness and water drain ×0.7. Eats and drinks alone (those trips may take her farther). Does not hunt; never picks up flint, bones or a burning stick; carries sticks and rocks. Fire fear starts at the inherited value + 20 and drops by 1 × 1.5 per warming near an adult.
- **Learning:** copy odds from someone within copy range are ×2 for a child, ×1.5 for a youth. A child who sees a parent or group member succeed gains half of what the success gave them (never past their score), without a copy roll. "X watched Y and copied: ...". The card shows "Learned from: Zol (fire), Lom (huddling)" as 44 px links.
- **Youth:** roams and follows like an adult, hunts with kill odds ×0.5, can't pair until 16.
- **Orphans:** a child whose guardian dies keeps close to the group member she likes most ("X keeps close to Y now"); with nobody she is on her own ("X is on her own now"). No rescue.
- **Drawing:** the Artist's `data/art/child_16.png` (same layout as founders, figure ~75%); without it the founder sprite is drawn at 75%, feet on the same line.
- **Save v14**, `?v=14`; v13 saves load (name counts rebuilt).
- **Director calls on 20:** at night a partner / group member / guardian / ward within 1.5 tiles adds +12.5 °C felt (half a fire's 25 °C hearth heat, `person.warmth.bondC`); in Deep Cold pair / group members lie down beside each other to sleep (`gatherSeason`); a Deep Cold night on which warmth fell under 40 (`nightFirstBelow`, latched for that night) skips bedtime food trips and hunger wake-ups. Climate unchanged. A stick lying within 1 tile (diagonals too) of a burning tile catches and becomes a burning stick (`fire.brand.catchTiles`). Fire score ≥ 40 (`braveScore`): a struck tree's flames are only kept away from within 1.5 tiles. Reach stays 3. A child may rub a held rock on flint. Death tables: `tests/deaths_v14.md`, `tests/deaths_v15.md`.
- **Sleep churn fixes (step 20):** hunger wakes a sleeper at most once a night; on a cold Deep Cold night food waits for morning but water below `actAt` still sends her to drink (before, she woke thirsty, could not want water while fullness was lower, and lay down again every minute).
- **Orphans (Director, 21):** when a carrier dies the baby lies alone. The group member who likes the dead carrier most walks over from up to `family.seekTiles` (15); with no group member that close, any awake adult within `anyTiles` (10) may go, rolling each hour with odds curiosity - fireFear (%). Whoever comes within `adoptTiles` lifts it ("<Name> lifts the baby and carries it"). A baby survives `orphanMinutes` (2 days) alone.
- **Sleeping warmth (Director, 21):** `person.warmth.sleepHeat` - 3+ people asleep within 1 tile of each other feel a fire's heat (25 C); 2 get half (12.5 C). It replaces the night bond warmth for sleepers. At warmth <= `urgentBelow` (20) a person heads for the nearest group member / partner / guardian or fire before anything else (water below `wakeIfNeedBelow` still wins; retried every `urgentRetry` min). The climate is unchanged.
- **Burning sticks (Director, 21):** an idle adult within `fire.brand.warmTiles` (1) of a burning stick lying on the ground rolls the hourly grab too (no flee check for a stick that close). `fireStats.caught` counts sticks lit by nearby flames, never people.
- **Prayer art (21):** `data/art/pray_16.png` / `child_pray_16.png` (row = founder look; kneel, arms up, kneel+arms up, sway L/R, front) draw a praying person; "still" uses the sleep row, an offering the kneel frame with the item in front, face / circle the walk rows. `prayer_spot_16.png` draws prayer spots on the map (few stones / more stones / cairn at 0 / 6 / 12 prayers there; `art.spot.at`).
- **Herd test with prayer off:** the step-16 herd test runs with `faith.hourDiv` huge, so its draw order matches v14 (prayer bouts shift the hunts on seed 7).

## Faith and prayer (step 21)
- **Trait** `faith` 0-100, rolled for founders, inherited with drift like the others; shown on the card.
- **When:** a need under 25 (`faith.needBelow`) or a death seen within 8 tiles in the last day: chance faith/400 per game hour to pray 10-40 min. Not a task; never instead of drinking/eating with the source within 5 tiles. Own rng stream (`prng`).
- **Forms:** 1-3 actions from kneel, raise arms skyward, sway, offer (a held berry/meat/bone/flint/stick, left on the ground), circle a spot, face the sun / fire, lie still; stored as strings like `kneel+offer:stick`. The first form is random, weighted ×3 for what is held / a fire in sight.
- **Spread:** an awake onlooker within 5 tiles may copy the form (learning copy odds × curiosity/50, ×1.25 group, ×2 child). A need that rises 10+ within 6 h makes that form liked (weight 1 + like/25): a superstition.
- **Spots:** 3 prayers within 2 tiles make a prayer spot (data only, not drawn); someone starting a prayer within 10 tiles walks there first.
- **Prayers tab** (next to Log and Discoveries): action lines only, newest first, group filter, last 300 lines, saved. No speech, no quoted words, no named powers. Per-seed numbers: `tests/prayers_v15.md`.
- **Save v15**, `?v=15`; v14 saves load (faith rolled per person from the seed, empty prayer state).

## Time and tuning (all in `data/config.js`)
| Thing | Value |
|---|---|
| Tick | 1 tick = 1 game minute = 5 real s at 1x (1 day = 2 real h). 10x = 2 ticks/s, 60x = 12 ticks/s |
| Loop | rAF plus accumulator, at most 240 ticks per frame, frame dt clamped to 0.25 s |
| Calendar | 7 days/season, 4 seasons (Greening, High Sun, Leaffall, Deep Cold; they set the air temperature and ground dryness) = 28-day year. Starts Day 1 06:00 |
| Day/night | Full light 07-18, full dark 21-04, with a fade between |
| Scale | All traits and needs are 0-100; traits: 50 = average; needs: 100 = good, they drain down. Traits are ints rolled as 50 + gaussian·17.5, clamped (full 0-100 allowed). Needs are stored as floats and shown rounded |
| Founders | The seed picks 5-8, ages 16-40, names from `names.json` (no repeats), placed on distinct land tiles within 3 tiles of a middle-meadow point. Founder i wears look i mod 8 of the Artist's `founders_16.png`. Two people never end a move on the same tile, and people only stop on whole tiles |
| Decision | Act on the lowest of fullness / water once it is below 50; a tie goes to water. Once she has set off for food or water she keeps that goal unless the other need drops below 10, except that water always wins: she never carries on to food (or explores for it) once water is the lower need under 50 |
| Traits | **speed**, **appetite**, **curiosity** (idle chance to pick up a thing in sight = 0.6 × curiosity/50; copying odds), **coldFear** (seeks warmth below warmth 35 + 0.5 × coldFear) and **fireFear** (odds of going near embers/fire and of copying it) |
| Walk | 8 / (0.5 + speed/100) game min/tile: 16 at speed 0, 8 at 50, 5.33 at 100. Crossing 60 tiles at speed 50 = 8 game h (1/3 day) |
| Fullness | 0-100, 100 = good. Hungry below 50. Drain = (85 − 50) / 360 per min × (0.5 + appetite/100), i.e. 5.8/h at appetite 50: from full (85) to hungry in 6 game h (12 h at appetite 0, 4 h at 100). Logs "<name> is hungry" when it drops below 50, and "<name> is starving" once at 0; holds at 0 until she eats; no death |
| Water | 0-100, 100 = good. Drains 100→50 (thirsty) in 4 game h, 0 at 8 h; no trait scales it. Logs "is thirsty" below 50 and "is parched" once at 0; no death. Drink from any land tile next to river/lake (4-neighbour): 10 min, water set to 100 |
| Energy | 100 = rested. Drains 100→0 over 16 waking h, refills 0→100 over 8 h asleep. After dusk (18:00-06:00) an idle person with energy < 50 lies down where she is (sleep sprite row + a small "z"); she gets up at dawn (06:00) once full. While asleep fullness and water drain at 1/4 rate; she wakes early only if one drops below 10 (then goes back to sleep that night). Energy 0 logs "is exhausted" once and she lies down after finishing her step, day or night |
| Bedtime | Before lying down, if water or fullness would drop below 25 by morning and water / a known ripe bush is within 6 tiles, she goes for a drink / meal first (once a night). The bedtime meal counts as overeating |
| Planning | From fullness 70 / water 75 down, before wandering off she leaves early if the nearest known food / water is far enough that she'd arrive below 50 |
| Meals | One ripe bush = one meal, eaten in one 15-min sitting, and the bush is then empty. A meal adds 40 fullness; a hungry meal stops at 85 (`mealFillTo`, "full"). Overeating (the bedtime meal, or a snack above 50 if `snackAbove` is set; off by default) adds the full 40, capped at 100 ("overfull" above 85). Logged as "<name> ate berries (word)": full, overfull, or the state she ends in (fed/hungry) when she started very low. About 2.2 meals, 3 drinks and 1 sleep per person-day in the 10-day balance run |
| Bushes | 60 (raised from 30 for 5-8 people), 85% ripe at start, at least 4 tiles apart. An eaten bush is ripe again after 1440 min (1 game day) in Greening, 18 h in High Sun/Leaffall, 36 h in Deep Cold (`bushes.season`). Full/empty use the Artist's `bush_full.png`/`bush_empty.png` |
| Sight | Fixed 6 tiles. Bushes are only known once seen (`knowledge.bushes`). She expects an empty bush back 1 day after she last saw it empty. Water tiles seen are remembered (`knowledge.water`) |
| Aging | A year is 28 game days; age rises 1/28 year per game day. Each person gets a seeded lifespan of 60-100 years and dies of old age on reaching it |
| Death | After 1 full game day (1440 min) continuously at water 0 ("of thirst") or 7 game days (10080 min) at fullness 0 ("of hunger"); the clock restarts as soon as the need is above 0. Energy 0 never kills. One log line per death, e.g. "Aru lies still. Aru has died of thirst." (`person.death`) |
| Bodies | No grave. Fresh 1 day, then rotting 7 days (flesh 100→0 by time, minus what scavengers eat), then bones tagged material "bone" (data only) that weather 100→0 over 20 game years and then crumble away. People ignore bodies. Bodies are inspectable and saved (`body`) |
| Grazers | 15 in 3 herds on meadow, away from the founders. Own fullness (100→0 over 1 day), graze 60 min on a meadow tile when under 50, wander/idle around their herd's spot, which drifts 3-6 tiles every 3 h. A person within 4 tiles: they run (3 min/tile) to 6+ tiles away and the herd re-centres there ("Grazers scatter from X"). No deaths or births (`animals.grazer`) |
| Scavengers | 4, small. Notice a body with flesh within 15 tiles when under 70 fullness, walk to it and feed (+1 fullness/min, each point takes 0.15 flesh), which cuts the rotting stage to ~2 days (10-seed mean 2.0, range 0.4-3.6) and leaves the bones. They avoid a body with a person near it and flee people the same way ("Scavenger N runs from X") (`animals.scavenger`) |
| Things | 40 rocks, 40 sticks (by the woods), 15 flint (by the river), seeded, one per tile; 4 bones can be taken from each skeleton (then it is gone). Fresh/rotting bodies cannot be picked up. An idle person may walk to a thing in sight and pick it up (one at a time), and puts it down when a need takes over, at bedtime, or after 1-5 h (`objects`) |
| Tries | With something in hand, she may strike / rub / cut it against a thing within 10 tiles (chance 0.8, at most every 6 h; right after a pick-up too). No learning or tool use yet: ~0.7 tries per person-day. Each try is logged as an action ("Aru strikes flint on rock: a spark") |
| Physics | Every result comes from the material table `data/materials.js` (hardness, sharpness, dryness, durability, burns) by the rules in `config.physics`; there are no recipes. Strike: both hardness ≥ 65 and an edge ≥ 35 → spark (60%); the softer whole thing may break into a piece whose edge = 1.4 × hardness ("sharp shard" from 60). Rub: abrades/sharpens the softer one; soft dry on soft dry grows warm. Cut: an edge ≥ 55 notches a softer thing. Rates: flint on rock 61% spark; bone on rock 23% sharp shard |
| Warmth | 0-100, 100 = warm. Air °C: season means 13 / 23 / 12 / −2 ± 5 by time of day (coldest 05:00). Felt = air + 5 under trees + 4 per huddled neighbour (max 3, within 1.5 tiles) + 3 sun by day + 2 walking − 1 asleep + fire heat. Below 10 °C felt she loses 0.85/h per °C, above it she gains 1.5/h per °C. Cold people (warmth < 35 + coldFear/2) walk to trees / others within 15 tiles and stay there; on a cold night they find such a spot before lying down, and wake once if cold in the open. Logs "is cold" / "is freezing". **A full day (1440 min) at warmth 0 kills ("of cold")**. Winter survival without fire: 24/24 founders in the headless year test and 38/40 (95%) over a year on seeds 1, 2, 3, 7, 42, 99 (2 cold deaths); 40/40 with fire on the same seeds |
| Fire | A spark lands on the target's tile and catches if the ground (or a stick there) is dry: dryness by season (meadow 35/70/55/25 ±25 per tile, −30 by water) over 45. Ember (20-60 min) → fire → spreads each minute to dry neighbours (p = 0.012 × dryness factor) → burns 40 min (meadow) / 120 (woods) → ash 1 day → scorched → regrown 6 days later (~1 season). Burns bushes bare, sticks away, bodies to bones; rock/flint/bone stay. People and animals move away (2.5 → 5 tiles); no burn damage yet. Forced fires in High Sun: 1-126 tiles, out in 0.7-9 h, regrown ~7 days after the start (`fire`) |
| Pop caps | 60 people / 40 animals (animals count toward 40). 5-8 founders spawn |

Person data model: `{id, kind, name, pos, age, look, needs{fullness, water, warmth, energy} (all 0-100, 100 = good), traits{speed, appetite, curiosity, coldFear, fireFear}, exp{fire|huddle|shelter: {score, n, burns, copied}}, carry (item id or null), knowledge{bushes, water}, relations, lifespan, zero{water, fullness, warmth} (minutes at 0)}`. Item: `{id, kind:"item", material, form (whole/shard), x, y, heldBy, props{hardness, sharpness, dryness, durability}}`. Discovery: `{n, key, action, materials, result, who, whoId, tick, group: ""}`. Bush: `{id, x, y, ripe, regrow}`. Animal: `{id, kind:"animal", species, herd, pos, facing, needs{fullness}, act, stats{flees, meals}}`. Body: `{id, kind:"body", of, name, look, x, y, diedAt, cause, age, stage, stageAt, flesh, eaten, condition, material, scavenged}`.

## Test hook (not in the UI)
From the browser console: `GS.debug.drainWater(id)` / `GS.debug.drainFullness(id)` hold that need at 0 (death follows after 1 / 7 game days; "is parched" / "is starving" logs once), `GS.debug.release(id)` stops holding, `GS.debug.ageTo(id, years)` sets an age (she dies of old age once it reaches her lifespan). `id` defaults to the selected person.

Names are drawn from a seeded shuffle with no repeats. After the list is used up, it reshuffles.
