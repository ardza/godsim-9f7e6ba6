# Director calls: steps 15b, 16, 17 (Oct 23)

## 15b Map variety (layout only, art and rendering unchanged)
- Every value below comes from the seed through its own random stream, so the same seed always gives the same map.
- Height field: 2-3 octaves of value noise. Hills are the top 10-15%, drawn as the current rock tiles in clusters.
- Moisture field: separate noise plus a per-seed bias from dry (-0.3) to lush (+0.3).
- Lakes: 0-4 per map. Fill low basins, and blob shapes are fine. Total water stays at 3-12% of tiles.
- Rivers: 0-2. Start high, walk downhill to a lake or the map edge, 1 tile wide (2 near the end).
- Forest: forest_density 0.1-0.6 per seed, placed by moisture. Bushes favor moist tiles, and dry seeds get 30-50% fewer.
- Guarantees: the founders' spawn has water within 12 tiles, there are at least 15 bushes, and all land is reachable on foot.
- Debug: GS.debug.seedInfo() returns lakes, rivers, water %, forest density, moisture bias.
- Proof: one composite image of 4 seeds side by side (dry, lush, river-heavy, lake-heavy) at tests/screenshots/seeds_15b.png.

## 16 Meat
- Grazers can be killed by a person who is holding a rock or stick and is low on fullness.
- Odds: base 10%, then +30% for a sharp item (flint or a bone shard) and +20% if the person has learned it.
- On success the grazer becomes a body (same stages as people), and meat can be taken from it while fresh.
- Raw meat: +60 fullness, with a 25% chance of a "sick" log line (energy -20). Meat is only edible while fresh.
- Learning: a hunt_score that copying can spread (as in step 14). No scripted "invented hunting" line, only data-detected firsts.

## 17 Cooking
- Meat carried next to an ember or fire for 30 game minutes becomes cooked meat, a new item state.
- Cooked meat: +70 fullness and no sickness.
- Discovery is detected from the data when someone first eats cooked meat. A cook_score then raises the odds of deliberately holding meat near fire, and it spreads by copying.
- Burnt: if it stays more than 120 minutes it becomes "charred", +20 fullness.

# Director calls: step 18 Relations and groups (Oct 23, 3:16 AM)
- Each person stores, for every other person seen: familiarity (0-100) and liking (0-100, starts at 50).
- Familiarity: +1 per game hour spent within 4 tiles of them, slow decay of -1 per game day apart.
- Liking goes up when you're near them while a need of yours rises (eating near them, warmed at the same fire, huddling): +2. It goes down when they take the bush, meat or fire spot you were heading for: -3. It drifts toward 50 at 1 point per 3 days.
- Following: when idle, a person drifts toward the most liked person seen within 15 tiles (if that liking is 60 or more), staying 1-3 tiles off.
- Groups: every game day, cluster people whose mutual familiarity and liking are both 60 or more (connected components). A group needs 2 or more people. A seeded color per group stays stable while most members are the same. Loners have no color.
- Group knowledge: copying odds +25% from a group member and -15% from a non-member. Each group's known skills are the union of its members' learned scores of 40 or more.
- Avoidance: liking under 25 makes a person keep 3 or more tiles away when idle. No fighting yet.
- On screen: a small ring in the group color under each person. On each card, a Relations section lists the top 5 by familiarity, with a liking word (warm 70+, neutral 40-69, wary under 40) and the numbers. The log gets "Quo and Zol now keep together" when a group forms, plus a split line. All of it is action-only, with no speech.
- Save v12, ?v=12. Test proof: a 4-seed composite screenshot with the group rings visible, plus the group counts over 1 year.

# Director calls: step 19 Reproduction and inheritance (Oct 23, 3:54 AM)
- Bond: two adults (age 16-45 years) with mutual liking >= 75 and familiarity >= 70 become a pair. One pair per person. Logged once as "Zol and Quo stay close by night now". A pair breaks if either liking drops under 40 or one partner dies.
- Conception: on a night when a pair sleeps within 1 tile of each other, both have Fullness >= 50, Water >= 50 and Warmth >= 40, and the population is under 60: chance 2% per night. One of each pair is set as the carrier at seed time (sex: a 50/50 seeded trait on every person, shown on the card).
- Pregnancy: 1 season (7 days). The carrier's fullness drain is x1.25. If the carrier dies, nothing is born.
- Birth: one baby beside the carrier. "Zol gives birth; a baby lies beside her" (use the person's set pronoun from the sex trait, or the name only).
- Inheritance: each numeric trait (curiosity, fear, fire_fear, lifespan, etc.) = average of the parents ±5% drift. A 2% chance per trait of mutating ±20%. Learned scores are NOT inherited (that's 20 childhood).
- The baby joins the parents' group and gets a seeded name from names.js.
- Babies (age 0-3 years, until step 20): they stay within 1 tile of the carrier (carried), and their needs are met when the carrier eats, drinks or sleeps. If the carrier dies, the baby dies after 1 day unless another adult in the group is within 2 tiles (adopted, logged).
- Aging: 1 year = 28 days, so babies only become kids after 84 days. Fine.
- On screen: a baby sprite (Artist: 8x8-ish, drawn smaller on the carrier's tile). The card shows Parents (clickable), Children, and inherited traits marked up or down versus the parent average. Births go in the Events filter.
- Save v13, ?v=13. Test proof: births over 1 year across 8 seeds, the cap holds at 60, and a trait-drift table for 10 babies.

# Director calls: step 20 Childhood (Oct 23, 4:43 AM)
- Age stages: baby 0-3 years (carried, as in 19), child 3-12, youth 12-16, adult 16+. With a 28-day year, a child stage lasts 252 days.
- Child (3-12): walks alone, keeps within 4 tiles of a parent (or an adopted adult) while awake, and sleeps beside them. Needs drain x0.7. Eats from bushes and drinks alone. Can't hunt or pick up flint or burning sticks. Can carry sticks, rocks and berries.
- Learning: copy odds from adults within 5 tiles are x2 for children and x1.5 for youths. A child who watches a parent or group member do any scored action gains that score at half the adult rate even without copying, so habits seep in. The log line is "Tib watched Zol and copied: ...", the same as for adults.
- Fear: a child's fire_fear starts at the inherited value +20, and each warming near an adult at fire lowers it (x1.5 the adult rate).
- Youth (12-16): roams freely but prefers the group. Can hunt at half kill odds. Can't pair until 16.
- Orphans: a child whose parents are dead attaches to the group member with the highest liking (logged). If there's none, they wander alone, which is risky. No scripted rescue.
- On screen: children drawn at about 75% scale (Artist: a child sprite strip like founders_16, 4 directions, or scale the founders in code if that's faster). Youths at full size. The card shows the Age stage and "Learned from: Zol (fire), Lom (huddling)".

# Director calls: step 21 Faith and prayer (Oct 23, 6:22 AM)
- Trait: faith 0-100, seeded for founders, inherited like the other traits.
- Trigger: a need under 25 (or a nearby death seen in the last day) gives a per-hour chance = faith/400 to start a prayer bout of 10-40 game minutes. This is not a task to do and it never replaces drinking or eating when the source is within 5 tiles.
- Ritual form = a sequence of 1-3 primitive actions picked from: kneel, raise arms skyward, sway or dance, place an item on the ground (offering: berry, meat, bone, flint, stick), circle a spot, face the sun or the fire, lie still. Each person's first prayer picks a random form, weighted by what they're holding and what's near.
- Copying: a prayer seen within 5 tiles is copied as a form, using the normal copy rules (group x1.25, child x2). Forms are stored as strings like "kneel+offer:berry".
- Reinforcement (no real effect, just coincidence): if the need that triggered the prayer rises within 6 hours, the person's liking for that form goes up and they reuse it. This lets superstitions emerge from data. Offerings stay on the ground as normal items (they rot or get eaten).
- Places: if 3 or more prayers happen within 2 tiles of the same spot, it becomes a "prayer spot" marker in the data (not drawn yet), and praying people drift toward it if it's within 10 tiles.
- Prayers tab in the bottom bar (with Log and Discoveries): a feed, newest first, of action lines such as "Ena kneels and raises her arms skyward, shivering" or "Tib places a berry on the ground and sways". A filter by group. It shows the last 300 lines and is saved.
- Card: Faith number and Prayer form(s) used, with a count.
- No speech, no gods named, no scripted religion.
- Save v15, ?v=15. Test proof: prayer counts per seed over 2 years, at least 2 distinct forms spread through copying on some seed, and a screenshot of the Prayers tab on desktop and phone.

- **Also in this freeze — Warmth equilibrium (Commissioner bug):**
  - Felt temp `feltC` = air °C + shelter + huddle + fire + activity bonuses (existing stack, still capped).
  - Neutral air = 10°C (50°F). Equilibrium: `eq = clamp(50 + (feltC - 10) * 2.5, 0, 100)`.

  - Each tick: move warmth toward `eq` by `min(abs(eq - warmth), rate)` where rate ≈ 0.8/min when |felt−neutral| is small, up to ~2/min when far (keep the old magnitude band, just retarget to eq instead of 0/100).

## 32. Carrying & caching (save v29, ?v=29) — LOCKED Oct 24 (Commissioner: build now)

- **Fix:** While rain **exposure > 0** (open-air active rain), do not let warmth climb from the eq path — clamp positive `warmthRate` to 0 (or apply lossX to gains the same way).
